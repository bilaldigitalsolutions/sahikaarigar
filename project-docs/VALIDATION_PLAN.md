## 🗣️ Worker Interview Questions (8 Quick Questions)

> **Important:** Labor chowk pe worker 2 min bhi nahi dega. Isliye sirf 8 sawal pucho.

### Quick Questions (2 min max)
1. Aapka naam kya hai?
2. Kaunsa kaam karte hain? (Skill)
3. Roz kaam milta hai ya kabhi kabhi?
4. Middleman ko commission dete hain? Kitna?
5. WhatsApp chalate hain?
6. Kitna charge karte hain per day/hour?
7. Kaunse areas mein kaam kar sakte hain?
8. Apna photo lagayenge online profile pe?

---

## 🗣️ Employer Interview Questions (10 Quick Questions)

### Quick Questions (3 min max)
1. Aapka naam kya hai?
2. Kaunse area mein rehte hain?
3. Ghar ka kaam karwane ke liye worker kaise dhundhte hain?
4. Worker dhundhne mein kitna time lagta hai?
5. Kabhi kharab experience hua hai worker ke saath?
6. Trust ka issue hota hai anjaan worker ko bulane mein?
7. Kitna charge dete hain normally?
8. Online worker dhundhne ka tarika chahiye?
9. Worker ka rating/review dekh ke hire karenge?
10. Kya female members ke liye safety zaroori hai?
14. Kitna pay karenge acche worker ke liye?
15. Kaunsa kaam sabse zyada chahiye? (Electrician/Plumber/etc.)

## 📱 WhatsApp Setup (Community + Broadcast)

> **Important:** 50 workers + 20 employers ek group me 200 msgs/day karenge. Koi nahi padhega.
> **Better:** WhatsApp Community ya Broadcast list banao.

### Option 1: WhatsApp Community (Recommended)
```
Community Name: "Sahi Kaarigar Hyderabad"

Announcement Group (Sirf tum post karo):
- Workers ke liye: Job announcements, tips
- Employers ke liye: New workers available

Sub-Groups (Members discuss karein):
- "Electrician Hub" - Electricians + Employers
- "Plumber Hub" - Plumbers + Employers
- "General Discussion" - Sab ke liye
```

### Option 2: Broadcast Lists (Simple)
```
Broadcast List 1: "Workers - Electrician" (50 members)
```
